# App Store metadata: Telugu

App Store Connect localization "Telugu". The English source and the field limits are in [APP_STORE.md](../APP_STORE.md). The terms follow [LOCALIZATION_GLOSSARY.md](../LOCALIZATION_GLOSSARY.md): "ఎటాక్" for attack and episode, as Telugu iOS keeps English loanwords, "మైగ్రేన్" for migraine, "నొప్పి" for pain, and "ట్రిగ్గర్" for trigger. The counts were made with Python `len()` and `len(text.encode("utf-8"))` on October 24, 2026.

### Keywords

85 bytes in UTF-8 (33 characters) of 100 bytes. A letter or sign of the Telugu script takes 3 bytes in UTF-8. The name and the subtitle are searchable already, so the list does not repeat them. "పార్శ్వపు నొప్పి" is the traditional Telugu name of migraine ("one-sided pain").

```text
తలనొప్పి,పార్శ్వపు నొప్పి,ఆరా,pdf
```

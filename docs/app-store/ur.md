# App Store metadata: Urdu

App Store Connect localization "Urdu". The English source and the field limits are in [APP_STORE.md](../APP_STORE.md). The terms follow [LOCALIZATION_GLOSSARY.md](../LOCALIZATION_GLOSSARY.md): "دورہ" for attack and episode, "مائیگرین" for migraine, as Jang and Urdu hospital pages write it next to "درد شقیقہ", and "محرک" for trigger. The counts were made with Python `len()` and `len(text.encode("utf-8"))` on October 23, 2026.

### Keywords

87 bytes in UTF-8 (50 characters) of 100 bytes. An Arabic, Hebrew or Urdu letter takes 2 bytes in UTF-8. The name and the subtitle are searchable already, so the list does not repeat "مائیگرین", "ڈائری" or "رپورٹس". "شقیقہ" is the Urdu word of "درد شقیقہ".

## Siri

Siri does not speak Urdu ([iOS feature availability](https://www.apple.com/ios/feature-availability/), Siri section, read on October 18, 2026). The description names the App Shortcut "دورہ درج کریں" in the Shortcuts app and Spotlight instead.

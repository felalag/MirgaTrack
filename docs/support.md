# MigraTrack Support

## Contact

Send an email to CONTACT_EMAIL. Tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version, and the steps that cause the problem.

## Where is my data?

Your data is only on your iPhone and on your paired Apple Watch. MigraTrack has no account and no server, so we cannot see or restore your data.
An iCloud or computer backup of your iPhone can include the MigraTrack data.

## How do I delete my data?

Open Settings › Delete All Data in the app. This permanently deletes all MigraTrack data on the iPhone. When you delete the app, its data is deleted too.

## The app does not show my sleep

MigraTrack reads sleep from Apple Health. In the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps › MigraTrack, and turn on Sleep.
Your iPhone or Apple Watch must record sleep first.

## The watch does not show my episodes

Open MigraTrack on the iPhone once. The iPhone then sends your recent episodes and your logging preset to the watch.
Keep Bluetooth on and the watch near the iPhone.

## Is MigraTrack medical advice?

No. MigraTrack is a diary. It does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease. Speak with your clinician before you change your treatment.
